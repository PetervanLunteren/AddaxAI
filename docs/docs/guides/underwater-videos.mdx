# Analyse underwater videos

AddaxAI analyses long underwater videos, such as baited remote underwater video stations (BRUVS). It follows each animal through the video, so you check a 90 minute drop animal by animal, not frame by frame. This page takes you from setup to counts.

A video tutorial is coming soon.

## Choose underwater

The first time you open the app, the setup screen asks what data you work with. Choose underwater. You can change it later in the View menu, under Data type.

The app then offers only underwater models. There is no species model for underwater use yet, so the AI finds the animals and you name the species.

| Model | Finds | Speed |
|---|---|---|
| SharkTrack | Sharks and rays | About half an hour per hour of video, on a laptop without a GPU |
| Community Fish Detector | Any fish | Nano is fast. Small and medium find more, but are several times slower without a GPU |

Both also analyse photos. See the [model zoo](../reference/model-zoo.mdx) for details.

## Put one drop in one folder

- Put the videos of one drop in one folder.
- In a project, add that folder as one deployment.
- GoPro chapters of one drop can stay separate files. They become one event, and the count covers all of them.

Use a folder run for one batch of drops, and a project when you keep adding drops. See [choose a workflow](../start-here/choose-a-workflow.mdx).

## Check the animals

On the Labels page, Detections shows one card per animal, at the frame where the AI saw it best. What you do with a card applies to every frame of that animal.

- Give the card a species, or press <kbd>V</kbd> when the label is right.
- Press <kbd>X</kbd> for a false detection, such as the bait, algae or debris.

Use Files to watch a whole drop. Under the video is a timeline with one bar per animal.

See [check the labels](./check-labels.mdx).

## Confirm the counts

The Counts page has one event per drop, with one row per species. The count is MaxN: the most animals of that species on screen at once. Each row shows when that count was reached and when the species first arrived. Click Show to see that frame.

Confirm the number or change it. To add an animal the AI missed, type the number, or draw it on the MaxN frame in Files.

The counts export has the MaxN time and the first arrival time. See [exports](../reference/exports.md).

## Good to know

AddaxAI was built for camera traps first. Some words and graphs, like trap nights and the rates on the dashboard, don't fit underwater work. Ignore what doesn't apply to your data. It doesn't mean your results are wrong.
