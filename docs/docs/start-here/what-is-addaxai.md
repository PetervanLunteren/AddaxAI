# What AddaxAI does

AddaxAI finds the animals in your camera trap photos and videos, and tells you which species they are. It runs on your own computer. Your files stay on your machine.

You give it a folder. It does three things:

1. **Find.** A detection model draws a box around each animal, person and vehicle.
2. **Name.** A classification model says which species is in each box.
3. **Check.** You look through the results and fix anything the AI got wrong.

After that you get your data out: tables, species folders, or a file for another program.

## What it does not do

- It does not analyse your data for you. You get clean tables and charts. The ecology is your work.
- It does not upload anything. There is no account and no cloud.
- It is not always right. Treat the labels as a first pass, then check them. See [confidence and verification](../understanding/confidence-and-verification.md).

## Two ways to work

You can point at one folder and get files out, or you can build a project that you keep adding to over time. They give different results, so pick before you start. See [choose a workflow](./choose-a-workflow.md).
